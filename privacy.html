Video Uploader privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Video Uploader is a personal tool used only by its owner to upload videos to their own YouTube channels. It uses YouTube API Services; the Google Privacy Policy also applies.

Google data it uses

  • Sign-in tokens, to upload videos to the channel that gave permission.
  • Channel ID and name, to check that the right channel is signed in.
  • Upload results (the video's link and privacy status), to show the result and avoid uploading a video twice.

Where it is stored

The long-lived sign-in token, channel ID, channel name and upload record (including the video link) are kept on the owner's computer. Login files there are readable only by the owner's user account. There is no Video Uploader server.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising or used to train AI models. Videos and their details are sent to YouTube to upload them. The tool has no analytics, ads or tracking.

Video Uploader's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Keeping and deleting data

Data is kept until the owner deletes the files on their computer. Access can be removed at any time in Google account permissions. Once access is removed, the stored token stops working and the stored Google data is deleted within 7 days.
