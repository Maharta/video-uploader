Video Uploader

Video Uploader is a small personal tool that its owner runs on their own computer to upload their own videos to their own YouTube channels. It is not offered to the public.

What it does

  • Uploads a finished video with its title and description to the owner's channel, as private, scheduled or public.
  • Reads the signed-in channel's ID and name, so a video can't go to the wrong channel.

It doesn't read viewers' data, comments or analytics, and it doesn't edit or delete videos.

Google access it asks for

  • youtube.upload: upload the owner's videos.
  • youtube.readonly: check which channel is signed in.

It uses YouTube API Services. By using it, the owner agrees to the YouTube Terms of Service.
